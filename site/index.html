How it works

One monitored brand. 36 buyer-phrase prompts you help curate. Zero scraping — official APIs only.

  1. An agent asks your 36 buyer questions across AI engines (Groq-hosted gpt-oss-120b and Gemini Flash today) — phrased the way buyers actually ask: “best tool for X”, “X alternatives”, “how do I solve X”.
  2. Every answer is parsed for citations — exact and alias matching for your brand and each tracked competitor, with the exact prompt and snippet kept as a receipt.
  3. Your share of voice is scored per question, per engine, and week over week — gains, losses, and competitor movement are diffed against last week.
  4. Every Monday you get the digest — page + email — ending in exactly 3 prioritized fixes with the data that justifies each one.

What this is and isn't

It is

  • Weekly monitoring of what AI assistants answer to your buyers' questions.
  • Scores built from official free-tier APIs (Groq, Gemini) — no scraping, ever.
  • Exactly 3 prioritized recommendations, each backed by this week's data.
  • Fully disclosed methodology — your exact prompts and scoring math ship inside every digest.

It isn't

  • A direct tap into ChatGPT or Perplexity consumer apps — LLM answers via official APIs are proxies for those engines. That's disclosed in every digest.
  • An SEO tool — we monitor AI answer citations, not Google rankings.
  • A guarantee of citations — it's a measurement and fixing loop, not magic.
  • A data broker — we track only the brand and competitors you list in your config.

If a proxy approach is a dealbreaker for you, wait until engine-native monitoring APIs mature — we'd rather say it plainly than oversell it. Transparency is the product's whole style.
